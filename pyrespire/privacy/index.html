Pyrespire Privacy Policy

Pyrespire is a 9+ fantasy tower defense game. We do not require an account. We do not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Optional ads are requested as child-directed / non-personalised (COPPA flag on). Optional in-app purchases go through Apple. We do not include a third-party analytics SDK in this version.

1. Who this policy is for

The game is rated 9+ and is not listed in Apple's Kids Category. Because children may play, we treat ad requests as directed to children under 13 (contextual ads, no behavioural tracking, no ATT / IDFA). If we ever listed the app in the Kids Category, we would have to remove the third-party ad SDK (Apple Guideline 1.3).

2. Information the game stores on the device

The game stores progress, settings, and your wallet (Embers, Cinder Gems, Remove Ads, cosmetics) in local save data on the device (and in iCloud/iOS backup if the user backs up the device). This data is used to run the game, not to profile you.

3. Advertising (Unity Ads via LevelPlay)

If you have not purchased Remove Ads, the game may show optional rewarded ads and occasional between-map interstitial ads from Unity Ads, mediated by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies). Banner ads are off. Ads are not shown in the middle of a wave.

We configure the Unity / LevelPlay SDK with a child-directed / COPPA flag so those partners should serve contextual, non-personalised ads and should not use IDFA to track you. We do not call Apple's App Tracking Transparency API and we do not include an NSUserTrackingUsageDescription.

Unity and its advertising partners may collect technical information (for example device type, OS version, IP address, coarse location derived from IP, ad performance identifiers such as SKAdNetwork attributions, and crash/diagnostic data) to deliver, measure, and fight fraud on ads. Their own policies apply:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads / LevelPlay documentation and partner list may be updated by Unity.

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase that turns off between-map interstitials. Optional rewarded ads may still be available if you choose to watch them.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your full Apple ID payment details. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to payment. Product grants (currency, Remove Ads, the Starter Bundle cosmetic) are stored in the on-device wallet and can be restored with Restore Purchases for non-consumable items.

5. Analytics

This version of Pyrespire does not include Unity Analytics, Firebase, or another third-party analytics SDK. If that changes, this policy and the App Privacy label will be updated before the change ships.

6. Crash / technical logs

If you install a development or TestFlight build, Unity or Apple may collect crash logs and device logs to fix bugs. Production store builds do not add a separate analytics telemetry SDK.

7. Children (COPPA / similar laws)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for profiling or behavioural advertising. Ad requests are flagged child-directed. We do not ask for a name, email, or account inside the game. Parents can use Remove Ads, Apple's Ask to Buy, and device-level Screen Time / purchase restrictions. Contact feignnarula@nexaplaytech.com about a child's data.

8. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising partners listed above may receive the technical data required to serve contextual ads and measure installs (including SKAdNetwork). We do not share data with a data broker, and we do not use the data to track you across third-party apps.

9. Retention and your choices

Local save data stays on the device until you delete the app or clear its data. To stop interstitial ads, buy Remove Ads or uninstall the app. For an Apple purchase or refund question, use Apple's report-a-problem flow.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if our practices change and will post the new date on this page. Questions: feignnarula@nexaplaytech.com.
